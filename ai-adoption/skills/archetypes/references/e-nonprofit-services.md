# Archetype E: nonprofit, incubator, or services organization

Any size. Program delivery, funder relations, cohort or member management. Also the archetype for accelerators and studios that will later run the program for portfolio companies.

## Emphasis

Funder reporting and program materials first. Both are high-volume, template-driven, and currently done by the most stretched people.

## Persona prior

Self-Starter program managers. Traditionalist board and finance. Skeptics among senior program staff who worry about tone and relationships.

## Default risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood |
|---|---|---|
| Confidential applicant, participant, or donor data in prompts | High | High |
| Tone drift in donor and community communications | Medium | Medium |
| Board discomfort with AI-assisted materials | Medium | Medium |
| Measurement Void: impact claims without baselines | Medium | High |

## Occupation candidates (Economic Index)

Fundraisers; Social and Community Service Managers; Training and Development Specialists; Public Relations Specialists; Bookkeeping, Accounting, and Auditing Clerks; Human Resources Specialists; Technical Writers.
