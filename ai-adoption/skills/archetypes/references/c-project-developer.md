## Emphasis

Compliance and reporting first (highest volume, most template-able); O&M documentation second. Start with the department whose head is a Self-Starter.

## Persona prior

Conformist-heavy. Traditionalists in field roles. Skeptics in compliance and legal, for good reasons.

## Default risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood |
|---|---|---|
| Regulatory submissions with unverified AI content | High | Medium |
| Uneven adoption between office and field | Medium | High |
| Personal data of landowners and communities in prompts | High | Medium |
| Department silos: each department reinvents the playbook | Medium | High |

## Occupation candidates (Economic Index)

Compliance Officers; Financial Analysts; Project Management Specialists; Environmental Engineers; Wind or Solar technicians (query "technicians"); Public Relations Specialists; Data Scientists; Paralegals and Legal Assistants.
