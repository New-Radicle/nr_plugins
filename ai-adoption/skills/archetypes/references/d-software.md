## Emphasis

Go-to-market content and customer research first; engineering is usually ahead and needs governance more than enablement.

## Persona prior

Trailblazer-heavy engineering. Conformist go-to-market. Expect the underground pattern in engineering: people already use AI heavily and have not said so.

## Default risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood |
|---|---|---|
| Underground usage in engineering with no shared standards | Medium | High |
| Crowdsource Trap: every engineer picks a different tool | Medium | High |
| Customer data in prompts | High | Medium |
| Go-to-market content that drifts off brand or overclaims | Medium | Medium |
