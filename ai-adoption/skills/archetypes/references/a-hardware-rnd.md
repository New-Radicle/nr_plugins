# Archetype A: grant-stage hardware R&D

Founder-led, 1-25 people. Revenue is grants, awards, and pilots. Physical work dominates. An operations function does not exist yet and one or two people cover finance, hiring, and communications on the side.

## Emphasis

Grants first, documentation second. The first three workflows are usually grant discovery, proposal narrative from technical notes, and grant reporting.

## Persona prior

Trailblazer founder. Skeptic senior scientist. Small Conformist middle. Expect the underground pattern among junior researchers.

## Default risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood |
|---|---|---|
| Hallucinated citations or claims in proposals and reports | High | Medium |
| Unpublished results or IP in prompts | High | Medium |
| Founder bottleneck: the Sponsor and Rollout Lead are the same overloaded person | Medium | High |
| Over-trust on safety-relevant technical content | High | Low-Medium |

## Occupation candidates (Economic Index)

Mechanical Engineers; Electrical Engineers; Materials Scientists; Chemists; Technical Writers; Grant-related: Fundraisers, Technical Writers; Purchasing Agents; Compliance Officers; Human Resources Specialists.
