## Emphasis

Knowledge management and engineering documentation first; commercial enablement second. Companies at this stage lose the most time to undocumented decisions and re-explained context.

## Persona prior

Full distribution. Expect Skeptics in senior engineering and manufacturing, Conformists across the middle, Trailblazers in software-adjacent roles.

## Default risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood |
|---|---|---|
| Over-trust on safety-critical specifications and test results | High | Medium |
| Tool fatigue from too many new systems at once | Medium | High |
| IP and customer data in prompts | High | Medium |
| Uneven adoption between office and floor | Medium | High |
