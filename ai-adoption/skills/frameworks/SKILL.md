## Principles that apply everywhere

1. Leadership-led, not mandated. The Sponsor uses the tools visibly before anyone is asked to.
2. The Rollout Lead owns execution. Nothing waits on the Sponsor except budget, licenses, and team-wide messages.
3. Craft-respecting. AI removes administrative load; it never replaces expertise. Say this in every workshop, playbook, and coaching conversation.
4. Evidence-based. Baseline in Week 0. If it is not in the scorecard, it did not happen.
5. Persona-aware. Different people need different first tasks. Persona data stays private to the leads.
6. Progressive trust. Low-risk, reviewable tasks first. Widen only after the first error has been handled well.
7. Visible, not underground. Wins are shared; hidden usage is surfaced gently, not punished.
