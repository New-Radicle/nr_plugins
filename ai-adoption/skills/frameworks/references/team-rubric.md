# Team rubric: seven primitives of team health

Score each 0-5 from observable signals. Assign a confidence (High: three or more clear signals; Medium: one or two; Low: inferred). Weighted composite drives the band label. Always present scores for calibration; the person running the program knows things intake does not capture.

| # | Primitive | Weight | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compelling Direction | 20% | Clear, motivating purpose everyone can state |
| 2 | Shared Mental Models | 15% | Common understanding of who does what and how decisions happen |
| 3 | Mutual Trust | 15% | Trust in competence, intent, reliability |
| 4 | Psychological Safety | 10% | Interpersonal risk without punishment |
| 5 | Adaptive Capacity | 15% | Ability to learn, pivot, respond |
| 6 | Communication Architecture | 10% | Information flow; knowledge captured and findable |
| 7 | Enabling Structure | 15% | Roles, processes, frameworks in place; critical functions staffed |

## Rubric anchors

For each primitive, 5 / 3 / 1 anchors. Interpolate.

**Compelling Direction.** 5: mission is clear, measurable, consistently communicated, externally validated; people can state it unprompted. 3: mission exists but is not translated to daily priorities; some cannot connect their work to it. 1: no clear direction.
Signals: consistency of how people describe the mission, external recognition, ability to attract talent, roadmap coherence.

**Shared Mental Models.** 5: everyone understands each other's roles and constraints; shared vocabulary; strong knowledge transfer. 3: leaders share models but it has not propagated; silos between functions. 1: people work at cross-purposes.
Signals: consistent process descriptions, cross-functional understanding, onboarding speed.

**Mutual Trust.** 5: confident delegation, positive intent assumed, long tenure, autonomy. 3: baseline trust; some over-checking; untested under pressure. 1: blame culture, high turnover.
Signals: delegation patterns, how mistakes are handled, information transparency, advisor and investor quality.

**Psychological Safety.** 5: documented practices; leaders model vulnerability; dissent welcomed. 3: safety is leader-dependent and undocumented. 1: people are afraid to make mistakes.
Signals: whether junior people speak up, post-incident behavior, how disagreements resolve.

**Adaptive Capacity.** 5: demonstrated pivots; active scanning; diverse perspectives; fast learning from failure. 3: adapts when forced, slow to notice. 1: rigid.
Signals: pivot history, diversity of backgrounds, willingness to kill projects.

**Communication Architecture.** 5: central knowledge base; documented decisions; new hires self-serve context; regular sharing rituals. 3: tribal knowledge; decisions live in chat threads. 1: critical information regularly lost.
Signals: knowledge base existence and use, documentation habits, frequency of "I didn't know that".

**Enabling Structure.** 5: clear roles, documented decision frameworks, sane span of control, critical functions staffed. 3: leaders defined, middle unclear, some functions missing and compensated informally. 1: nobody knows who decides.
Signals: defined roles versus actual headcount, unfilled functions, single points of failure.

## Composite and bands

Composite = sum of (score x weight).

| Composite | Band |
|---|---|
| 4.5-5.0 | Thriving |
| 3.5-4.4 | Developing, strong foundation |
| 2.5-3.4 | Emerging, building blocks in place |
| 1.5-2.4 | Forming |
| 0-1.4 | At risk, foundational gaps |

## Why it matters for adoption

Communication Architecture and Enabling Structure are the two primitives AI adoption moves fastest, because documentation, knowledge capture, and process definition are the most AI-addressable work. Set month-3, month-6, and month-12 targets for those two first. Psychological Safety and Mutual Trust predict how the first error will be handled; low scores there mean the Sponsor must script the response before Week 1.

## Calibration patterns

People tend to score Psychological Safety higher than their team would, and Communication Architecture lower than an outsider would. Compelling Direction is the easiest to validate from public material. Record what changed in calibration and why.
