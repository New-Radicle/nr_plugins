# Adoption personas

Five personas, plus one hidden pattern. Map every person to one. Typical share of a team is a prior, not a rule; the archetype file adjusts it.

| Persona | Typical share | Motivation | Risk | What works |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trailblazer | 15-20% | Experimentation, edge | Races ahead; creates gaps; may hoard know-how | Go first, document what works, pull one Conformist along, present at office hours |
| Self-Starter | 15-20% | Autonomy, mastery | Disengages if it feels mandated | Docs and space. No group training. Ask them to write one playbook. |
| Conformist | 25-30%, the largest group | Alignment with leadership and peers | Assumes AI is optional if leaders do not visibly use it | Visible Sponsor usage, clear role playbooks, social proof. This group decides whether adoption reaches critical mass. |
| Skeptic | 15-20% | Proof, security, protecting quality | Rational concerns get dismissed as resistance | Start with the task they hate most, never the one they love. Frame them as the quality gate. Give 90 days. |
| Traditionalist | 7-15% | Stability, predictability | Left behind entirely | Pair with a Trailblazer. Guided, step-by-step. Simplest use case. Normalize mistakes. |

## The underground pattern

Roughly one in five people use AI without telling anyone. Signals: channel is quiet but license usage is high; polished outputs appear faster than before; people deflect when asked how they did something. Response: make usage visible and celebrated, never audited. Ask Trailblazers to post first so it is safe.

## Persona migration

The program's culture metric is people moving one step: Skeptic to Self-Starter, Traditionalist to Conformist. Track it privately in `personas.private.md` and report only counts on the scorecard, never names.

## Privacy rule

Persona assignments are written only to `personas.private.md` in the state folder. They are never posted to a channel, sent by email, or written to a shared database. When a skill needs persona data for a shared output, it uses counts per persona and nothing else.
