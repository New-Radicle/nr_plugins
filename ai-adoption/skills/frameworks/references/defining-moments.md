# The three defining moments

Prepare the Sponsor and Rollout Lead for these before Week 1. How leadership responds decides the program.

## 1. The first error

Sometime between Week 3 and Week 8, AI will get something materially wrong and someone will catch it.

Wrong response: "See, it doesn't work." Also wrong: hiding it.

Right response, said publicly: "Good catch. This is exactly why we have review. AI drafts, experts verify. The system worked." Then log it in the shared error log and thank the person who found it.

## 2. The craft challenge

A respected expert says: "I don't need AI to write my reports. Mine are fine."

Right response: "You're right, they are. Imagine producing them in a fraction of the time and spending the rest on the work only you can do." Then give them the task they hate, not the one they are proud of, and let them be the quality gate.

## 3. The invisible win

Someone uses AI for something that was previously impossible, not just faster: a document nobody had time to write, an analysis nobody could run, a proposal that would not have been submitted.

Right response: make it visible to the entire team within the week. This is the moment adoption stops being a program and becomes how the company works.
